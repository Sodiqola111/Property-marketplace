About the Platform

A professional marketplace concept for connecting property seekers with agents, landlords and property owners.

Purpose

The platform is designed to make property discovery, listing and enquiries easier through a clear digital marketplace experience.

How it works

  1. Property seekers search and filter listings.
  2. They open a property detail page and review available information.
  3. They can save a property or submit an enquiry.
  4. Agents and landlords use the listing form to submit properties.
  5. A future backend can handle verification, moderation and communication.
